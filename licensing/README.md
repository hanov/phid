## Choice and limitations

| Model | Fit for the supplied business goal |
|---|---|
| PolyForm Noncommercial + negotiated commercial terms | Selected by the stub; permits stated noncommercial purposes while reserving uses outside that grant. |
| MIT / Apache-2.0 | Permit commercial uses; do not implement the requested noncommercial business boundary. |
| GPLv3 / AGPLv3 | Permit commercial use subject to their conditions; copyleft does not require buying a commercial license for every business use. |
| Business Source License | Different model involving change-date licensing; not adopted here. |
| Custom source-available terms | Would require a separately reviewed license if the owner needs restrictions incompatible with PolyForm's express permissions. |

The exact standard license controls. It expressly permits use by listed
organizations, including health organizations, regardless of funding. The
original stub's blanket statements about every commercial or healthcare
organization were therefore too broad. If the business goal is to charge every
health organization, this selected standard license does not establish that.

The original stub also included nonstandard Profit Motive and Combined Work
Exception clauses, omitted Fair Use, and changed cure-period language. Those
alterations were removed by using the upstream text unchanged. Project-specific
copyright information is in NOTICE rather than inserted into the standard text.

The repository notice setup is ready for distribution. A commercial transaction
still needs completed business terms, authorized signatures, and counsel review;
a repository template is not an executed agreement. Record explicit contributor
acceptance before merging outside contributions. Verify actual dependency and
model licenses for every distributed bundle; the project license does not change them.
